# Product Guidelines: HEMA Training Tool

## Design Philosophy
This application is a tool for focused training. The design must prioritize clarity, speed, and reliability. Distractions should be minimized to allow the practitioner to focus entirely on their physical movements.

## Prose Style
- **Concise:** Use short, punchy sentences. Avoid unnecessary adjectives or lengthy explanations.
- **Instructional:** Focus on clear, direct commands (e.g., "Start", "Reset", "Next").

## Tone
- **Formal:** Adopt a professional and serious tone suitable for martial arts practice.
- **Authoritative:** Instructions should be unambiguous.

## Visual Aesthetic
- **High Contrast:**
    - Use stark contrasts (e.g., black/white, red/blue) to ensure visibility from a distance.
    - Large, legible typography.
    - Clear visual indicators for active vs. inactive states.
- **Minimalist:** Remove any decorative elements that do not serve a functional purpose.

## UX Principles
- **Speed First:**
    - The path from launching the app to starting a drill should be as short as possible.
    - Controls must respond instantly.
    - Avoid complex menus or hidden settings.
- **Feedback:** Provide immediate visual confirmation for all user actions.
